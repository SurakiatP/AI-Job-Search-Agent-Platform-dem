## What is getting started

This page takes you from creating an account and confirming your email to making your first project. A project is the space that holds the CV, jobs and documents for one job hunt. You can use it as soon as you have an account. No special permission is needed.

## How to get started

1. Click {{ui:"Create an account"}} on the login page.
2. Enter {{ui:"Email"}} (required).
3. Enter {{ui:"Password"}}, 10 to 128 characters (required).
4. Click {{ui:"Sign up"}}.
5. Open the email we sent you.
6. Click the link in the email, or enter the 6-digit code on the screen and click {{ui:"Verify email"}}.
7. Log in with your email and password.
8. Click {{ui:"Create project"}}.
9. Enter {{ui:"Project name"}} (required).
10. Click {{ui:"Create project"}} again.
11. The overview page of your new project opens.

## Good to know

- The code in the email expires after 10 minutes. The link can be used only once.
- You must confirm your email before you can use some features, such as asking the agent to do work.
- If you cannot find the email, check your spam folder. Or log in and click {{ui:"Resend email"}} in the bar at the top.
- A project name can be up to 200 characters long.
- Use one project for each kind of job hunt. The data in each project is kept separate.
- You can delete a project only when it has no CV, jobs or agent history.

## Problems and fixes

- Problem: "Email is not set up yet"
  Fix: The server cannot send email yet. Ask your administrator to turn on email sending, then try again.
- Problem: "This link did not work"
  Fix: If you already confirmed your email, just log in. If not, click {{ui:"Request a new link"}}.
- Problem: "A project with this name already exists. Use another name or open the existing one."
  Fix: Enter a different name, or open the existing project.
