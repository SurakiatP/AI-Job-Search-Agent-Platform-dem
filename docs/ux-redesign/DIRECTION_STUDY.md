# Visual direction study

Date: 2026-10-16  
Status: Design alternatives for owner selection. No direction has been approved or implemented.

This study supports [REDESIGN_PLAN.md](REDESIGN_PLAN.md). Product facts are confirmed in [PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md). The brief pins a calm, professional productivity experience, readable Thai/English, accessible states, retained capabilities, and the current frontend architecture. Indigo continuity is recommended; detailed visual choices remain open.

## Product mechanism and usage scene

The central mechanism is a **traceable CV/job revision pair that leads to reviewed application material and an accurate tracking record**. A person should see their next action without losing that identity. The representative scene is checking progress after work on a laptop and reviewing a job or pending input on a phone.

Impeccable's Operate rules apply: each reference world contributes type, palette, density and one signature treatment. Familiar navigation, controls and task-based work areas stay recognizable. A physical reference is not a reason to turn the application into a paper facsimile, map, poster, album sleeve or television wall.

The category rut to avoid is a generic metrics-card dashboard with a crowded sidebar and several equally weighted calls to action. Its opposite, an expressive scrapbook/AI spectacle, would also miss the daily task.

## Exploration record

Seven concrete sources were explored across personal records/editorial, physical wayfinding/exhibition systems, and public information displays. This is the exploration inventory, not seven competing choices for the owner:

- **Professional application dossier:** recognizably personal and careful; indigo/white, workhorse bilingual type, clear material/readiness hierarchy. Expression: a preparation strip connecting a chosen pair to its next task.
- **Thai bilingual editorial index:** readable hierarchy across mixed scripts; ink/slate and a limited accent, concise headings and aligned captions. Expression: compact opportunity rows with readable source notes.
- **University library wayfinding:** scope and destination are clear before detail; cool neutral ground, distinct indigo zone, restrained signs. Expression: stable project context and grouped destinations.
- **Bilingual exhibition labels:** title, author/source and explanation have separate levels; quiet surfaces, precise spacing, short captions. Expression: a concise result/evidence panel with clear attribution.
- **Rail timetable information:** comparable items and state changes align; disciplined density, strong text labels, a reserved service colour. Expression: an application list whose stage/time/source are easy to scan.
- **Airport departure information:** attention follows an explicitly named state/action; dark/light information bands, tabular numbers, high readability. Expression: an attention queue grounded in actual pending inputs and approvals, with no invented countdowns.
- **Collection catalogue:** identity, accession/revision and history remain attached to each item; cool white, indigo context, compact metadata and ordinary readable type. Expression: the source label reused across evaluations and documents.

The Impeccable exploration selected Collection Index as the lead proposal. Personal Dossier remains the single preferred familiar alternative. No selection was made on the owner's behalf; there is no approved direction contract or new DESIGN.md.

## Three complete proposals

### Collection Index

**Thesis:** Keep the next useful job-search action and its source identity in the same reading path.

**Palette/type/density:** Cool white work surface, indigo project rail, dark Thai text, 28px page title, 16px body and 14px aligned work rows. Selected scope, ordinary links, state colours and primary actions have distinct roles.

**First viewport:** Standard left navigation; project title and activity count; one attention action; a broad opportunity/evaluation list; a narrower named-CV context panel. Counters are secondary. A source label sits on the selected work item and in its detail.

**Signature:** The same compact source-label treatment identifies named CV/revision ↔ job/revision, followed by a real history/source link. It does not certify unsupported facts or invent provenance that the API does not provide.

**Visitor path and reach:** Open the relevant opportunity → read the exact pair/result → choose one preparation task → return to an accurate tracking record. The label scales to session headers, document cards/history and approval detail.

**Risk:** Excessive provenance can make the interface feel archival and push the next action down. Keep only essential identity in the main view and reveal technical IDs in Details.

### Personal Dossier — Impeccable's pick

**Thesis:** Present a personal job-search workspace around readiness and the next preparation task.

**Palette/type/density:** Indigo context header, white work area, soft cool grouping, the same Thai type system with a more generous top module.

**First viewport:** Standard project navigation, a prominent readiness/continue panel, a short workflow summary, current applications/evaluations and recent materials below. The CV context is inline with the task.

**Signature:** A concise preparation strip names the selected pair and the next available action, without pretending the whole search is a forced linear wizard.

**Visitor path and reach:** Prepare CV → inspect opportunities → continue a selected application. The strip becomes a compact task header inside Search/Session/Documents.

**Risk:** This is the most familiar productivity-SaaS direction and the one many redesigns would choose. Distinction depends on coherent content, typography and trust details, rather than an unusual visual structure.

### Clear Signal — competitive alternate

**Thesis:** Use one clear typographic hierarchy and a visible selection state to make dense work easier to read.

**Palette/type/density:** Navy/indigo context band, cool white panels, larger but bounded Thai titles, flat rows and strong text status. The Metro-inspired material is translated to restrained indigo; cropped headlines, live tiles and panorama navigation are excluded by the brief/Operate rules.

**First viewport:** Familiar project shell, a strong project/attention header, a dense current-work list and a clear selected-item context panel. All important names and actions remain visible without hover.

**Signature:** One continuous selection band connects the active opportunity row and its inspector using colour and written identity, with no 3D tilt or information-hiding animation.

**Visitor path and reach:** Scan stage/status → select item → inspect pair/evidence → act. Selection treatment works in search, tracking and revision history.

**Risk:** Strong colour and oversized headings can use too much space or feel louder than the brief. Limit the display scale and keep text/actions clear in both themes.

## Challenger evaluation

Each reference was first translated into real product content, then judged on **audience identification** and **product clarity**. Aesthetic novelty alone was not a winning criterion.

| Challenger | Verdict and case | Discipline retained in the lead proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metro typographic system | **Competitive:** direct type/state hierarchy can improve product clarity; lower identification with a careful personal dossier. Kept as Clear Signal | A competitive alternative stays visible rather than being silently absorbed |
| Orienteering map | **Declined on both axes:** a terrain/course visual language adds interpretation to a CV/job decision even when controls remain standard | An unambiguous legend: name score methods and state meanings rather than depending on colour |
| Saville catalogue sleeve | **Declined on both axes:** coded labels and extreme sparse black surfaces obscure the actual job, revision and action | Strict accent discipline: selection/primary action have explicit roles, and ordinary text stays readable |
| Streaming title-card wall | **Declined on both axes:** jobs need comparable written facts; poster-heavy/hover expansion weakens scanning and phone access | Deliberate selection emphasis without hiding essential metadata or dimming the rest of the work |
| Cassette J-card | **Declined on both axes:** handwriting and folding/side metaphors add irrelevant reading and navigation work | Finite task groups with clear boundaries; preparation is disclosed one task at a time |
| Variable-font specimen | **Declined on both axes:** giant type contrasts and specimen controls do not express the applicant's work and reduce useful density | Explicit typography metrics and Thai/mixed-script review; no clipped essential labels |

These transfers are improvements in system discipline, not copied textures, glyph codes, paper borders or costume controls. The ordinary category-standard option remains available at full quality if the owner prefers familiar convention.
