Findings
- none is not safe for tailor (trap claimed in 10/10, q 56) nor extract (q 70); fine for apply and ocr. low costs the same as medium but qwen-low tailor has 1 invented-number result and ocr had 2 zero-score pages (1 error, likely the 90 s timeout; thinking 1.2k tokens/page).
- medium is the best qwen setting overall (94.9, all traps 0 except draft 1) but p50 is 4-8x luna's (concurrent run, still large); practice ~100 s.
- Cost: qwen with thinking on (low/medium) spends 4-6k reasoning tokens per call and costs MORE than luna on every task except ocr (-16% to -166% saving); only thinking-off qwen is cheaper (-68% to -85%) and it is unsafe for tailor/extract/practice. Realistic saving is the chosen mix below (about 21%, quality 97.4 vs 96.9), not the headline 76% of all-none.
- Chosen per task (cheapest config within 3 quality points of luna and no extra trap/invented result): evaluate=qwen none, extract=qwen low, tailor=qwen medium, apply=qwen none, ocr=qwen none; draft and practice stay on luna. Not applied to .env. Confirm on the holdout before adopting (evaluate none relied on a repair round in 1/10 cases).
